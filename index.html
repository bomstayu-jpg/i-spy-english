Report writing / file 03

A Description of Personal Experience

A good personal-experience paragraph tells the reader about something that happened to you. It includes details, feelings, and a clear ending.

Writing mission: Describe an interesting, surprising, exciting, or frightening experience. It can be real or imagined, but write as if it happened to you.

Step-by-step guide

  1. Understand the task. Underline the topic words. Ask: What experience am I describing? When and where did it happen?
  2. Brainstorm ideas. Write notes: who was with you, what happened first, what happened next, and how you felt.
  3. Plan your paragraph. Beginning: introduce the event. Middle: describe the important events in time order. Ending: explain the result and your feelings.
  4. Use useful time expressions. For example: one day, last summer, at first, suddenly, after that, later, in the end.
  5. Add details. Use adjectives and sensory details. Explain what you saw, heard, or felt.
  6. Check your language. Check past tenses, subject–verb agreement, spelling, capital letters, and punctuation. Use linking words such as because, so, but, and although.
  7. Improve your work. Replace repeated words, add one interesting detail, and make sure every sentence supports the topic.

Useful sentence starters

  • I will never forget the day when…
  • It happened when I was…
  • At first, I thought…
  • Suddenly, I noticed…
  • I felt … because…
  • In the end, I learned that…

Writing checklist





Writing task 1 — The unexpected discovery

You found something unusual while you were doing an ordinary activity. Describe what happened. Write 80–100 words. Include where you were, what you found, what you did, and how you felt.

Writing task 2 — A day I will never forget

Describe a day when something surprising or exciting happened to you. Write 100–120 words. Include an introduction, events in time order, your feelings, and an ending.

Final editing challenge: Underline three time expressions, circle two adjectives, and check that your story has a clear beginning, middle, and end.
